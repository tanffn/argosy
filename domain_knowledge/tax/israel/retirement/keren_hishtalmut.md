# Keren Hishtalmut (קרן השתלמות)

Tax-advantaged savings with conditional withdrawal eligibility, not a blanket exemption for every deposit or withdrawal. Corrections await full-file re-verification; the old verification stamp is historical.

## Employee track — 2026

- Standard tax-favoured employer/employee rates are up to 7.5% / 2.5%, subject to the employment arrangement and contribution conditions; the employer contribution must not exceed three times the employee contribution. Employee contributions come from net pay, not a universal pre-tax deduction.
- Qualifying wage ceiling: ₪15,712/month (₪188,544/year). At 10% combined contributions this means **₪1,571.20/month or ₪18,854.40/year of contributions**, NOT ₪188,544 of contributions.
- Excess contributions can exist. Employer excess can be taxable employment income when contributed; gains attributable to deposits above the beneficial ceiling are generally subject to 25% tax at withdrawal. Do not tax employee net-pay principal a second time.
- Normal liquidity is after six years; three-year routes require statutory retirement age or qualifying training conditions. Maturity alone does not exempt excess-deposit gains.
- A withdrawal can close that account to new deposits. Do not promise that deposits after a withdrawal inherit its old clock. Verify provider account and seniority-transfer rules.

## Self-employed track — distinct limits

For 2026, deductible contributions are subject to 4.5% of qualifying income, income ceiling ₪293,397 (about ₪13,203 maximum deduction). The separate beneficial-deposit ceiling for exempt investment gains is ₪20,566/year. There is no additional blanket 7% deduction. Combined employee/self-employed cases need their own calculation.

## Use in recommendations

Calculate employer benefit, tax, fees, liquidity and risk for the actual account. The former “47% instant return” shortcut was unsupported: comparing contribution rates with an income-tax bracket does not establish a universal return. Do not recommend switching provider solely because of one year's relative performance; compare like-risk tracks, fees, longer evidence and transfer consequences.
